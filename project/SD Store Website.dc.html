What is the difference between an Eau de Parfum, an Eau de Toilette, an attar and a body mist? {{ s0 }}

Eau de Parfum carries the highest oil concentration of our sprays, so it sits closest to the skin and lasts longest. Eau de Toilette is lighter and fresher, good for daytime and hot weather. Attars are oil-based and alcohol-free, applied a drop at a time to pulse points, and they wear for the longest of all. Body mists are the lightest of the four, meant for a quick refresh after a shower or through the day.

What are impression fragrances? {{ s1 }}

Impressions are our own blends inspired by well-known designer scents. They are not the original houses and we never sell them as such. They are an affordable way to wear a profile you already love, at our own concentration and wear time.

How long will the fragrance last on me? {{ s2 }}

Expect 8 to 12 hours from our Eau de Parfums and attars, and 4 to 6 from an Eau de Toilette or mist. Skin type and weather change this, so spray on moisturised skin and on clothing at the collar to make it hold longer.

Do you ship across Pakistan, and can I pay on delivery? {{ s3 }}

Yes. We deliver nationwide in 2 to 4 working days and cash on delivery is available on every order. Gift packaging is included at no extra cost. Tell us on WhatsApp if it is going straight to the recipient.
